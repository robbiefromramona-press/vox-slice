Copyleft, in plain English

One copyleft piece is used: @breezystack/lamejs (LGPL-3.0), the MP3 encoder. The LGPL is a "weak" copyleft license. You can use Vox-Slice and the MP3s it makes freely, including commercially: the license covers the encoder's code, not your audio. The condition is that if anyone changes the encoder itself and shares that version, they must share their changes under the same license, and anyone who ships the encoder must let people swap in their own copy of it. Vox-Slice is fully open source (MIT) and pulls the encoder in as an ordinary, unmodified package, so anyone can rebuild the app with a different or updated encoder.

JSZip is offered under "MIT or GPL-3.0-or-later" and Vox-Slice takes the MIT option, so the strong GPL copyleft does not apply. No other GPL or copyleft dependency is used.

This is a good-faith summary, not legal advice. The license texts themselves are what count.
